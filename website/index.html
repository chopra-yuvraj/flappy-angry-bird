Playable Characters

Select between three distinct characters on the title screen using Tab or S.

Red

Classic

The standard character. Balanced flight dynamics, standard gravity response, and red feather particle effects.

Chuck

Speedster

Aerodynamic triangular body with a top crest. Responsive lifting power and agile flight dynamics.

Bomb

Heavy

Matte black body with an animated head fuse spark and expanded shockwave particles.

Mechanics and World

Engineered with custom physics, procedural asset synthesis, and responsive controls.

Defense

Energy Shield

Collect cyan shield orbs to surround your character with an energy barrier that absorbs one fatal collision.

Bonus

Golden Stars

Spinning stars spawn in obstacle gaps granting +3 bonus score points and particle effects.

Visuals

Day, Sunset & Night

Sky transitions dynamically from daylight to amber sunset and starry midnight as your score increases.

Depth

Parallax Horizon

Layered distant mountain silhouettes and drifting clouds provide atmospheric visual depth.

Audio

Procedural Audio

Real-time oscillator synthesis produces retro sound effects without external audio file latency.

System

High Score Storage

Personal bests and achievement medals are persisted locally in the browser.
